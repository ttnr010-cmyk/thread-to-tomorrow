The Life Cycle of a Cotton T-Shirt

A cotton T-shirt passes through several stages before reaching the person who wears it. Its journey begins with growing cotton and continues through processing, manufacturing, transportation, use and eventually its end of life.

Understanding the journey

Every stage of a T-shirt's life cycle involves different people, resources and processes. Exploring these stages helps us understand the environmental effects behind the clothes we wear.
